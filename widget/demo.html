How it works

Three steps from first call to a finished room.

  1. Design call

    A 30-minute video call to talk about your garden, your work and your budget. Free, no obligation.

  2. Site visit and drawings

    We measure the plot, check access and ground conditions, and send drawings and a fixed quote.

  3. Build

    Our own crew builds the studio on a prepared base, then connects power and data.

Questions

How do I book the design call?

Use the booking assistant: tell it when suits you, pick one of the open times, and you get a confirmation with the date, the time and your time zone. You can reschedule or cancel in the same chat.

Which time zone are the times in?

Yours. The assistant names the zone next to every time it confirms. If you say where you are, it uses that; otherwise it uses your browser's time zone and tells you so.

Do I need planning permission?

It depends on where you live, the size of the studio and how close it sits to the boundary. We check this with you on the design call.

Ready to talk about your studio?

Pick a time for a free design call. It takes about a minute.
